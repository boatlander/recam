ReCam

Old Phone, New Webcam

ReCam turns the iPhone or iPad you already own into a sharp camera for your Mac. Your Mac treats it like any other camera, over Wi-Fi or a USB cable. Pair once, then pick ReCam in your video app.

  • Wireless or wired. Plug in a USB cable for the most stable connection.
  • Switch lenses and set focus and exposure from your Mac while you're live.
  • Free at 720p. ReCam Pro, a one-time purchase, adds 1080p, 4K and 60 fps. No subscription, no ads.
  • Private by design. Video goes only between your own devices, over an encrypted link.

ReCam is two free apps: one for iPhone and iPad, and one for Mac from the Mac App Store.
